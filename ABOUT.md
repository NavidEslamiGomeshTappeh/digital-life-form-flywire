# Digital Life Form — FlyWire

Version 1.7.1 is the current unified public baseline.

The project focuses on exact neuron identity, cross-layer provenance, deterministic reconstruction, claim-level evidence, artifact integrity, and reusable scientific evidence packages. It is not a claim to have reconstructed the complete fly brain and it does not assign biological compartments where the available evidence does not support that claim.

The canonical cross-chat and agent context is maintained in [PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md).
